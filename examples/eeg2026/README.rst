NM000134 EEG-to-image competition companion
============================================

This gallery contains one real NM000134 EEG-to-image competition companion:
``tutorial_eeg_to_image_start_kit.py``. It uses the BIDS EEG recording and
image-event mapping for sub-09, ses-01, task-images, run-02 to validate a
real BIDS event, epoch, and stimulus mapping before the linked NeuralBench
workflow.

The tutorial is not a competition score or split. NeuralBench owns the
official competition split, model, scoring, and submission workflow.
